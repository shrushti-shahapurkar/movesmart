🩺 Further Assessment Information

This page describes differences between recorded gameplay scores. The comparison dataset has not been established as a reference sample of typically developing children.

A score below the dataset average does not establish that a child has a medical condition or needs medical treatment. Game performance may be affected by practice, instructions, device differences, and the way each game calculates scores.

If parents or teachers have persistent concerns about a child's everyday development, learning, attention, or coordination, they may discuss those concerns with a pediatrician or qualified child-development professional. Do not delay seeking advice just because game scores appear average or high.
